Cookie Policy

How KITCHPRO Uses Cookies

KITCHPRO uses cookies and similar browser technologies to support site functionality, remember visitor preferences, and understand how this kitchen remodeling comparison platform is used.

Last updated: June 7, 2026

1. Overview

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how KITCHPRO may use them, and how visitors can manage cookie preferences.

This policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Platform note

KITCHPRO is an independent comparison platform. It does not perform kitchen remodeling work directly and does not guarantee independent provider pricing, availability, licensing, insurance, or project results.

2. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files stored on a visitor’s device by a website or browser. They can help websites remember preferences, keep certain features working, and understand basic usage patterns.

Similar technologies may include localStorage, sessionStorage, pixels, tags, and browser identifiers.

3. How KITCHPRO May Use Cookies

KITCHPRO may use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • To remember cookie consent choices.
  • To support website functionality and page navigation.
  • To improve user experience across pages.
  • To understand general website usage and performance.
  • To help maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical issues.

4. Types of Cookies We May Use

Essential Cookies

Essential cookies or similar storage technologies help the website function properly. These may support navigation, security, form behavior, or cookie preference storage.

Preference Cookies

Preference cookies may remember choices such as cookie consent decisions or website display preferences.

Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies may help understand how visitors interact with the website, which pages are visited, and how site performance can be improved.

Marketing or Partner Cookies

If used, marketing or partner cookies may help understand referral sources or support provider comparison operations. Availability may depend on current platform integrations.

5. Cookie Consent and LocalStorage

The cookie banner on this website stores a visitor’s Accept or Decline choice in localStorage under a cookie preference key. This helps prevent the banner from appearing repeatedly after a visitor makes a choice.

localStorage is stored in the browser and can be cleared through browser settings or developer tools.

6. Third-Party Technologies

Some cookies or similar technologies may be provided by third-party services used for hosting, analytics, security, communications, or platform operations.

KITCHPRO is not responsible for the cookie practices of third-party websites, independent providers, or external links.

7. Your Cookie Choices

Visitors can manage cookies in several ways:

  • Use the website cookie banner to accept or decline non-essential cookies where available.
  • Change browser settings to block, delete, or restrict cookies.
  • Clear localStorage and cached site data in browser settings.
  • Use browser privacy tools or extensions to control tracking technologies.

Blocking certain cookies or storage technologies may affect website functionality or user experience.

8. Updates to This Cookie Policy

KITCHPRO may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised last updated date.
