Quote factors

Questions to Ask About Island & Layout Quotes

Use this table to compare island and layout provider quote details before choosing a path.

What to compare Why it matters Ask providers
Clearance and flow Island placement affects movement, appliance access, and comfort. What clearance is planned around the island and key work zones?
Seating plan Seating needs can affect overhang, support, size, and countertop quote details. How many seats does the island support, and what overhang is included?
Storage features Drawer banks, pull-outs, and cabinet base choices can change pricing. Which island storage features are included or optional?
Utilities Electrical, plumbing, cooktop, or sink changes can significantly affect scope. Are outlets, lighting, sink, cooktop, or plumbing changes included?
Countertop support Large islands and overhangs may need support planning and special fabrication. What countertop thickness, edge, support, and seam plan is included?
