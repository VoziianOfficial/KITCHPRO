Quote factors

Questions to Ask About Lighting & Finishing Quotes

Use this table to compare lighting, fixtures, hardware, and finishing provider quote details.

What to compare Why it matters Ask providers
Lighting type Different lighting zones affect function, mood, installation needs, and cost. Which lighting types, locations, and controls are included?
Fixture brands Fixture quality, availability, and warranty can vary widely. What fixture brands, finishes, and model options are included?
Electrical or plumbing scope Moving lights, sinks, outlets, or faucets may affect scope and timeline. Are electrical or plumbing changes included or separate?
Hardware count Pulls, knobs, handles, and hinges can add cost based on quantity and finish. How many hardware pieces are included, and what finish is specified?
Finish matching Mixed finishes can look intentional or mismatched depending on coordination. How are faucet, lighting, hardware, and cabinet finishes coordinated?
