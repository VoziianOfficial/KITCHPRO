Contact FAQ

Questions Before Sending a Request

Helpful details about quote requests and provider comparison.

Does submitting the form hire a kitchen remodeling provider?

No. Submitting the form does not hire a provider. It helps organize your request so you can compare relevant provider options.

What information should I include in the message?

Include the project type, rough timeline, materials you like, current kitchen concerns, and any quote questions you want clarified.

Can I request options if I am not sure what service I need?

Yes. Choose “Not sure yet” and briefly describe the project. The request can still be organized into a likely service category.

Does KITCHPRO guarantee provider pricing?

No. Pricing, availability, licensing, insurance, warranties, and project terms are handled by independent providers.
