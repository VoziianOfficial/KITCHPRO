Privacy Policy

How KITCHPRO Handles Information

KITCHPRO is an independent kitchen remodeling provider comparison platform. This policy describes how information may be handled when visitors use this website, submit a contact form, call, email, or request provider comparison options.

Last updated: June 7, 2026

1. Overview

KITCHPRO helps homeowners explore kitchen remodeling service categories and request quote options from relevant local provider paths. KITCHPRO does not perform remodeling work directly and does not act as a contractor.

By using this website, you agree that information you provide may be used to respond to your request, support platform operations, and improve the comparison experience.

Important platform note

Providers referenced through this platform are independent. Homeowners are responsible for verifying licensing, insurance, quote terms, warranties, and project details before hiring any provider.

2. Information We May Collect

KITCHPRO may collect information that visitors voluntarily submit through forms, phone calls, emails, or other contact methods.

  • Contact details, such as name, email address, phone number, and ZIP code.
  • Kitchen project details, such as service category, message content, preferred timeline, and quote questions.
  • Technical information, such as browser type, device type, pages visited, referral source, and general usage data.
  • Cookie and preference data, such as cookie consent choices stored in the browser.

Do not submit sensitive personal information through the contact form. The form is intended only for kitchen remodeling comparison requests.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to operate the website, respond to requests, and support the provider comparison process.

  • To respond to contact form submissions, phone inquiries, or email requests.
  • To help organize kitchen remodeling service categories and quote needs.
  • To connect or route requests toward relevant independent provider options where applicable.
  • To maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website experience.
  • To comply with applicable legal, regulatory, or operational obligations.

4. Information Sharing

KITCHPRO may share submitted information when necessary to respond to a visitor request, support the comparison process, operate the website, or comply with legal obligations.

  • With independent local providers or service partners when a visitor requests quote options.
  • With website, hosting, analytics, communication, or operational service providers.
  • When required to comply with law, protect rights, prevent fraud, or support security.
  • As part of a business transfer, restructuring, or platform ownership change.

KITCHPRO does not guarantee the work, pricing, availability, licensing, insurance, or performance of any independent provider.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to remember preferences, support basic site functionality, understand website usage, and improve the user experience.

The cookie banner stores an Accept or Decline choice in localStorage so the preference remains available across pages. You can learn more in the Cookie Policy.

6. Your Choices

You may choose not to submit information through the website. You may also contact KITCHPRO to request that contact information submitted through the platform be reviewed, updated, or removed where applicable.

  • You can decline non-essential cookies through the cookie banner.
  • You can adjust browser settings to block or delete cookies.
  • You can contact KITCHPRO regarding submitted information.
  • You can choose not to proceed with any independent provider option.

7. Data Security

KITCHPRO uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information submitted through the website. However, no website, internet transmission, or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Third-Party Links

This website may include links to third-party websites or services. KITCHPRO is not responsible for the privacy practices, policies, content, or actions of third-party websites or independent providers.

9. Children’s Privacy

This website is intended for adults seeking kitchen remodeling provider comparison information. It is not intended for children, and KITCHPRO does not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Policy Updates

KITCHPRO may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised last updated date.
