Quote factors

Questions to Ask About Full Kitchen Remodeling Quotes

Full kitchen quote paths can vary widely. Use this comparison table before choosing a provider.

What to compare Why it matters Ask providers
Layout scope Layout changes can affect timeline, labor, materials, and overall quote range. Does the quote include layout changes or keep the current footprint?
Cabinet plan Cabinets can be a major cost and design factor in full kitchen projects. Are cabinets new, refaced, custom, semi-custom, or stock?
Surface materials Countertop and backsplash materials affect durability, care, and final appearance. Which countertop, backsplash, and finish materials are included?
Removal and cleanup Disposal and cleanup may be included or handled separately by providers. Is removal, disposal, and cleanup included in the quote?
Warranty and terms Warranty details and payment terms help compare providers beyond price. What warranties, deposit terms, and payment schedule apply?
