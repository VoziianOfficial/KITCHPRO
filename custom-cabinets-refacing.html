Quote factors

Questions to Ask About Cabinet Quotes

Use this table to compare cabinet provider quote details beyond the final price.

What to compare Why it matters Ask providers
Cabinet method Refacing, replacement, and custom cabinets can have different costs and timelines. Is the quote based on refacing, new doors, new boxes, or custom cabinetry?
Materials Box material, door material, finish type, and hardware affect durability and look. What cabinet materials, finishes, and hardware are included?
Storage features Pull-outs, inserts, pantry systems, and drawer upgrades can change pricing. Which storage upgrades are included or optional?
Measurements Accurate measurements help avoid fit issues, delays, and quote changes. How are measurements confirmed before final ordering?
Warranty and finish care Paint, stain, and hardware warranties can vary by provider and material. What warranty applies to cabinet materials, finish, and hardware?
